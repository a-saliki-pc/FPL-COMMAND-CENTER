❓ How it works

1. Your data lives only on your device. Nothing is sent to a server. If you clear your browser, you lose your plan — use Export to back up.

2. Everyone shares the same URL but has their own squad. Your family can open the site on their phone/laptop and enter their own Team ID. Your plans never mix.

3. Grabbing your squad is a two-tab trick. Open FPL, run a one-liner in the console, drop the JSON file into the tool. The console snippet is provided on the main page.

4. Why the trick? Browsers block direct API calls to FPL from other sites (CORS). Running the fetch from FPL's own site bypasses this cleanly — no server, no proxy, no cost.
